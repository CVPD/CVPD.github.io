**Dr. Clément Caporal** is a postdoctoral research fellow at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), working in the Computer Vision and Pattern Discovery (CVPD) group.

Previously, he earned his PhD in Computer Science from Ecole Polytechnique, France, under the supervision of Anatole Chessel and Emmanuel Beaurepaire on large scale **brainbow analysis**. Then, he worked as a postdoc in the Pasteur Institute, France, in the group of Roberto Toro, studying the effect of **brain folding** on cell shape.

His research focuses on **bioimage analysis**, where he develops **deep learning models** and **visualisation tools** for the segmentation, detection, and analysis of microscopy images, with applications in **neuroscience** and **cell biology**.
