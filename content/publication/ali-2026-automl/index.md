abstract: Automated machine learning (AutoML) is transforming how predictive models
  are developed, yet its real-world performance compared to expert-engineered pipelines
  remains underexplored. This study systematically evaluates AutoGluon AutoML against
  classical machine learning using diabetes prediction as a representative clinical
  case. Across binary (100,000 Kaggle samples) and 3-class ADA 2025 experiments, AutoML
  matched expert pipelines in predictive accuracy (AUC0.9790 vs 0.9791) while reducing
  development effort by 93%, requiring minimal technical expertise. Statistical analysis
  confirms no significant performance difference. Beyond efficiency gains, AutoML's
  automated pipeline construction demonstrates potential for integration into emerging
  Agentic AI systems. These findings position AutoML as a practical, scalable, and
  democratizing tool for clinical decision support and broader data science applications,
  highlighting both immediate utility and future avenues for autonomous, high-impact
  predictive modeling.
